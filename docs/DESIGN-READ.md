## Design Read

**Delivery: BUILD.** The direction was given — Ferrari's own campaign, a fixed
copy deck, fixed assets. Offering three concepts instead would have been a way of
not doing the work.

```
Reading this as a retailer campaign page for existing and prospective Purosangue
buyers in metro Detroit, leaning cinematic-restraint automotive editorial.

Mandate: REDESIGN — Ferrari's identity is fixed and carries through untouched:
  the prancing-horse + OFFICIAL / FERRARI DEALER lockup exactly as the sibling
  retailer sites use it; the retailer name in #A6A6A6 uppercase in the position
  that lockup gives it; Ferrari's near-black ground; Rosso as the single accent;
  tracked uppercase grotesque as the only display voice; the copy deck's wording
  verbatim; the brochure renders uncropped in their own 16:9 frame.
  Structure, composition, hierarchy, interval and surface craft are in scope.

Style mode: DIRECTED HYBRID — anchor auction-editorial / contrast brief-derived
  Ferrari corporate / signature none. Unifying principle: the page presents a
  COLLECTION, so each composition is a catalogue entry and not a campaign frame.

Dimensionality: ABSENT — the assets are studio renders with no scene and no depth
  to stage; depth is carried by tone, interval, and the match between the page's
  ground and the render's floor.
```

**Why auction-editorial is compatible here, named rather than assumed:** the
deliverable is literally a *collection* of numbered Atelier compositions, each
carrying a livery and leather specification. That is a catalogue record — the
exact content shape this dialect exists for. It was not reached for because
nothing else was specified.

## Art direction — CONTROL MAP

Dialects are mixed by responsibility, never by page section.

```
CONTROL MAP
composition / grid        → auction-editorial      masses before components, composed asymmetry, downward exhale
hierarchy / density       → auction-editorial      rank from space and scale before ornament
typography voices         → Ferrari corporate      one grotesque; roles by case, tracking, size, ink
spacing / rhythm          → auction-editorial      4px scale, sections bottom-heavy 1:1.5 → 1:2
colour / contrast         → Ferrari corporate      near-black ground, Rosso the single accent
image behaviour           → auction-editorial      the plate: uncropped, dissolved, its record beside it
containers / geometry     → auction-editorial      hairlines, no boxes, zero radius
depth / materiality       → auction-editorial      tone and interval only
motion / interaction      → auction-editorial      motion seasons — here it does not even season
information presentation  → auction-editorial      the spec plate as the record

ANCHOR    auction-editorial   — 8 of 10 domains
CONTRAST  Ferrari corporate   — typography voices and colour
SIGNATURE none

COLLISION RISKS
  Ferrari's corporate register is centred, symmetric and loud at campaign scale;
  auction-editorial is asymmetric and quiet. If the Ferrari layer leaked past
  typography and colour into composition, the page becomes a centred hero with a
  subtitle and two buttons — anti-patterns D1, executed well and still generic.

COHERENCE GUARDRAILS
  the section-language ledger read DOWN its columns · the device budget (two
  devices only: the plate's pool of light, the hairline record) · Gate 2's
  structural detector, which measures rendered geometry and reads no class names.

REJECTED ALTERNATIVES
  PURE Ferrari corporate — would produce the full-bleed studio render with centred
    white type, which these assets cannot carry (C22): there is no scene in them.
  immersive-authored-world — no scene exists in any supplied frame; MAIN
    dimensionality would have nothing to stage.
  technical-luxury as anchor (the Rimac / Semler register) — its spec density is
    wrong for a two-item collection whose complete factual content is five colour
    names.

WHY THIS DIRECTION FITS
  concept   — a collection reads as a documented set rather than as two adverts.
  hierarchy — the record's own register separates facts from prose, so the page
              has two reading modes instead of one undifferentiated voice.
  Brand character is untouched: it stays Ferrari's.
```

## Composition Read

```
1.  Context        one retailer, two Atelier compositions, fixed manufacturer copy,
                   ten studio renders and no landscape photography whatsoever.
2.  Artistic image a plate book: a collection presented as records, lit one at a time.
3.  Format forces  16:9 assets against a 100svh first screen and a 390-wide phone;
                   scroll as sequence; a fixed masthead travelling over a dark cover.
4.  Major masses   one dark authored field · one lit plate carrying the car ·
                   one compact high-contrast identity mass · one quiet index-and-action
                   cluster · then, down the page, one paragraph alone in air,
                   one typographic pair, two plate-and-record chapters,
                   one working surface, one deeper closing band.
5.  Centres        SEMANTIC governs — the car in its pool of light. Made by isolation
                   and by value: it is the only lit thing on a near-black field.
                   The identity mass is a strengthening secondary centre, not a rival:
                   it holds the opposite corner and its rank mass is a third of the car's.
6.  Dominance      dominant the plate; subordinate the identity; support the index,
                   the action and the record; the field is the bridge between them.
7.  Balance        intentionally off-centre and stable: the car's mass low-left is
                   counterweighted by the identity high-right, with the index and
                   action closing the diagonal.
8.  Direction      the car's nose points right, into the type; Ferrari's own framing
                   left more air on that side. The eye runs badge → headline →
                   down the interval → index → action, and the car's direction returns
                   it. It exits down the page, not off the right edge.
9.  Rhythm         act · read · read · browse · browse · act · close. Two chapters
                   of the same kind, told in opposite orders, with a pause before them
                   and a working surface after.
10. Negative space the interval between the headline and the index is the page's
                   held breath; the empty lower-left quarter of entry 01 and the
                   lower-right of entry 02 are the chapters' releases, and they mirror.
11. Tension        the car sits low and left against a much lighter type mass high and
                   right; the counterweight is the index-and-action cluster beneath it.
                   Resolved, not left open.
12. Spatial depth  none constructed. Foreground/ground separation comes from value
                   alone: a lit pool on a graded near-black field.
13. Edges          the plate bleeds past the left edge and dissolves before it gets
                   there, so there is no edge to see. Interiors commit to a hard
                   rectangle instead — the one deliberate contrast in edge language.
14. Unity          every chapter is the same two ingredients — a plate and a record —
                   at different scales, orders and axes.
15. Typography     one family. Display as mass, tracked labels as texture, the record
                   as a ruled grid. No second voice anywhere.
16. Imagery        the renders' internal force is a single repeated camera angle;
                   the page answers it by changing scale, order and axis instead of
                   pretending the images vary.
17. Responsive     the two-column cover recomposes into a stacked one whose plate is
                   re-centred on the car's own centre, not on the frame's.
18. Functional     the visitor came to enquire about a car they cannot configure
                   themselves. Means listed below and verified present in the render.
19. Diagnosis      the opportunity and the risk are the same thing: the assets are
                   records, not scenes. Treat them as records and the page is
                   authored; treat them as scenes and it is a stretched photograph.
```

## Composition Plan

- **Mass scheme** — five on the first screen (field, plate, identity, index, action);
  three per chapter below.
- **Primary centre** — semantic, the lit car; secondary centres strengthen.
- **Hierarchy mechanism** — value and isolation first, scale second, ornament never.
- **Centre of gravity** — below and left of the optical middle on the cover; above
  centre in each chapter, with the exhale beneath.
- **Static or dynamic** — static. The composition must survive a screenshot.
- **Eye path** — badge → headline → interval → index → action → down.
- **Density distribution** — the record is the only dense region; everything else is air.
- **Rhythm and intervals** — chapter hairline, top pad `clamp(4rem, 8vw, 6rem)`,
  bottom pad `clamp(6rem, 14vw, 12rem)`; 1 : 1.5 → 1 : 2 at every width.
- **Negative-space strategy** — one held interval on the cover; one mirrored empty
  quarter per chapter.
- **Tension and counterweight** — low-left mass against high-right type, closed by
  the action cluster.
- **Image ↔ typography** — the record never sits on an image; the plate never carries
  type. They are neighbours, never layers.
- **Sectional development** — cover (event) → manifesto (pause) → heritage (argument)
  → 01 (image-first) → 02 (record-first, mirrored) → enquiry (working surface) →
  colophon (close).
- **Edge and cropping** — nothing is cropped. Exteriors dissolve; interiors commit.
- **Responsive recomposition** — stated per format, not inherited.
- **Functional realisation** — the means list below.
- **Measurable commitments** — recorded in `site/.gates/declare.json` and checked in
  the render by Gate 1: no horizontal overflow at either viewport, smallest type ≥ 14 px
  at both, type-scale ratio ≥ 2.5 on desktop, page ≤ 8 screens.

## Task and means

The means no compositional argument may delete, all verified present by
`site/.gates/product.json`:

1. the primary enquiry action, above the fold at every width;
2. the enquiry form and its submit;
3. a route to each of the two compositions — the cover index;
4. the retailer's identity, in the masthead and again in the colophon;
5. a secondary route out;
6. the record — the colour facts — for each composition.

## Selection lines

Every decision names what it strengthens and the specific intended effect.

| decision | strengthens | effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the plate's elliptical mask | concept | the studio rectangle stops being a container, so a configurator render reads as a lit object on the page rather than as a photograph pasted into a box |
| the pool of light living inside the plate | hierarchy | the light travels with the car, so the eye finds the subject before it finds anything else, and no layer edge can land on the section's ground |
| the hairline spec record | concept | the colour names read as a specification rather than as adjectives, which is what makes the page a collection and not an advert |
| the alternating axis | hierarchy | the chapters are told apart before they are read |
| entry 02 read record-first | concept | the two chapters are the same two ingredients in a different order, so the page develops instead of repeating |
| one accent, one role | brand character | Rosso marks where the action is, and the marque's red reaches the page through the car itself, where it belongs |
| interiors keep a hard frame | concept | an exterior is an object on a floor; an interior is a view. Two treatments, one rule |
| the retailer name as live 14 px text | usability | the visitor can read which dealer this is; at logo scale it would have shipped at 12 px |
| the enquiry form | usability | the page offers an action and now provides it |
| no scroll motion at all | brand character | the composition holds as a still, which is what a catalogue is |

**Read down the columns.** Typography voice never changes. Containers are
hairlines everywhere and boxes nowhere. Depth and motion are constant. Ground
changes exactly once — the colophon goes a step deeper, which is the book's back
cover. Image treatment has two values, by the stated rule. The signature device
appears only where there is an exterior to light. Asks vary and never run three
the same.

## Critique panel

**1 · Composition critic.** The central idea is one sentence: the collection is a
plate book, each composition a catalogue plate with its record beside it. The
devices that strengthen it are the mask, the record, the alternating axis and the
two chapter formulas. **One device competes:** the AGT badge is a light metallic
rectangle with its own rounded frame, in a page otherwise made entirely of air and
hairlines — it is the single imported container, and it wins attention as an
object rather than as an eyebrow. It stays, because it is Ferrari's own campaign
mark and removing it removes the campaign's identity, but it is the page's weakest
edge. No section belongs to a different art direction. Roles are not confused with
voices: one family throughout, differentiated by case, tracking, size and ink. On
removing 20% — the four captions were the answer, and they are gone; nothing else
goes without loss, since the index is the only route to the chapters and the
record is the content.

**2 · Craft critic.** Contrast measured on the composited render, not on the
tokens: 43/44 runs at 1440 and 42/43 at 390, none below floor, minimum **5.77:1**.
Pairs: ink 17.5:1 · ink-80 11.7:1 · ink-55 6.3:1 · retailer name 7.9:1 ·
white on Rosso 8.3:1, hover 6.0:1 · control edges 3.8:1. Smallest type 14 px at
both viewports; type-scale ratio 3.23 on desktop. Zero magic numbers, one 4 px
scale, internal gaps everywhere smaller than external. Reduced motion: 103 visible
elements in both states, zero with motion under reduce. Keyboard: all 14
interactive elements reachable in document order, 2 px `#f3f4f6` ring at 17.5:1.
No horizontal overflow at 1920, 1440, 900, 390 or 320.

**3 · User advocate.** "What is this" — answered on the first screen at every
width, 0 taps. "Show me the cars" — 1 tap from the index or a scroll; each chapter
gives exterior, interior and the exact colour record. "Talk to someone" — 1 tap
from three places, four fields. **The friction on the fiftieth use:** there is no
way to see the two exteriors side by side. A buyer choosing between Rosso Racing
and Verde Menthe has to scroll between them.

**4 · Brief advocate.** All four modules the deck specifies are present and the
copy is verbatim. The added form serves the deck's own line — *"Primary CTA:
ENQUIRE"* — which asks for the action and supplies no destination. Form has not
begun to lead function: the mask removes a rectangle and adds nothing, and the
record it sits beside is unstyled fact. One deck line is unserved: *"Media:
https://app.box.com/s/ylfjl4u6…"*, which is unreachable.

**5 · Contrarian.** The strongest case against, stated while agreeing with the
result: **this is a well-built argument for assets that do not support it.** The
copy promises coastal cliffs, alpine summits and deserts; the visitor is shown a
grey studio floor twice. Every craft number passes and the emotional claim of the
copy is never delivered by a single frame. The alternative most worth considering
is not a layout change: **one landscape frame per composition**, licensed or
requested, giving the heritage chapter a full-bleed environment plate. Second-best,
if no photograph can be had: name the campaign's ten American landscapes as a
typographic index, so the page says *the collection is a map* instead of implying
photographs it does not have.

### Disposition

| point | disposition |
| --- | --- |
| the AGT badge is the page's one imported container | **accept** — kept, named as the weakest edge, flagged to Alex |
| no side-by-side comparison of the two exteriors | **reject** — the deck presents two compositions as a sequence, not a chooser; a comparison view would invent a product feature the campaign does not have. Revisit if Cauley wants a configurator entry point |
| the hero media is unreachable | **accept** — substituted with Cauley's own lead composition, which is a defensible hero and not a placeholder; the Box asset drops into `.plate--cover` without changing the composition |
| the assets do not deliver the copy's landscape claim | **accept** — this is core, so `defer` is not available. Declared as the composition's asset dependency (C17) in the gate declaration; the structure is built to carry the page without the photography; and the one action that would close it is put to Alex now rather than at delivery |
| a typographic index of the ten landscapes as a fallback | **reject** — content invented to fill a composition (CP7). It needs Ferrari's copy first |

## Invariants applied — and where they bit

- **C22 / C9** — the mask geometry came from measuring the car, not from taste, and
  was verified on the delivered render at both viewports.
- **C17** — the asset dependency is declared, and a second mechanism (structure,
  interval, tone) keeps the page recognisable when the imagery weakens.
- **C14** — the enquiry form exists because the page offers "Enquire".
- **C3** — entry 02's interior was cut from 82% to 50% of the shell after the reduced
  full-page read: at 82% it was the brightest and largest mass and beat the cover.
- **U17** — bit hard. The first build faded the plate to `--bg` over a non-uniform
  ground and left a visible rectangle. Fixed by masking the image and moving the
  pool inside the plate.
- **U19** — bit. At 320 the masthead lockup, and at 1440 a caption, were overflowing
  into `overflow-x: clip`. Found by comparing every element's scrollWidth to its
  clientWidth, not by looking.
- **U13** — the masthead keeps a scrim for the whole length of the cover and takes an
  opaque ground only after its bottom edge clears the cover's.
- **U16** — every declared decorative layer was sampled and confirmed non-uniform;
  the cover's bottom edge samples exactly `#0d0e11`, so the ground hands over with
  no seam.
- **I7** — bit. The retailer name at logo scale would have shipped at 12 px; split
  into a mark plus live 14 px text.
- **I6** — the whole contrast sweep, measured on the composited render.
- **CP7** — The Mountains' record carries two rows, not three, because the deck names
  no livery for it. Nothing was invented to square the two chapters.
- **U7** — mobile drops the `OFFICIAL FERRARI DEALER` sub-line and the "The
  Collection" nav link. Both are re-composed rather than dropped: the sub-line is in
  the colophon, and the route is the cover index sitting directly below.

## Dialect yields — each with its condition

| rule | condition | what was done instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| typography **D1** three-voice system | *the brand has its own type system* | Ferrari runs a single grotesque. One family; the label register is carried by case, tracking, size and dimmed ink |
| typography **D2** italic signature word | *the brand voice is deliberately flat or technical* | none used |
| typography **D3** mono micro-labels | same condition | tracked uppercase grotesque at the 14 px floor |
| colour **D2** desaturated accent | *the brand is genuinely colour-led* | Ferrari Rosso `#a00909`, measured from the supplied badge |

Not yielded, applied in full: typography D4 (spec plates), colour D1 (dark-first,
never `#000`/`#fff`), colour D3 (accent under 5% and in named roles — here, one),
spacing D1–D4, anti-patterns D1–D10. The page carries **no** content motion at
all, which is stricter than motion-taste D1/D2 rather than a yield.

## Judgment calls

- The masthead is a campaign nav — *The Collection* and *Enquire* — not a
  reproduction of Cauley's full site navigation. Dead links to New Vehicles,
  Pre-Owned and Service would be worse than one short honest bar.
- Below 360 px the masthead drops its action entirely; the cover's own ENQUIRE is
  above the fold at that width, so nothing is lost.
- Captions were removed everywhere rather than kept on the interiors only, to avoid
  a second inconsistency while fixing the first.
- Archivo as the single family, verified in the render at 22 / 24 / 28 / 40 / 46 px
  on both grounds with no stroke loss (I10). Ferrari Sans is not licensable here;
  Archivo's uppercase proportions sit closest to the retailer lockup's own.
- The cover's hero is Cauley's lead composition rather than a generic frame, so the
  substitution for the missing Box asset is defensible rather than arbitrary.

## Known compromises

- The gate chain runs on the system Chrome, not Playwright's Chromium, which has no
  mac13-arm64 build. Gate modules unmodified; only the binary differs.
- `hero.humanConfirmed` and Gate 3's nine answers are deliberately unfilled. They
  are a person's to give, and the chain correctly refuses Gate 5 until they are.
- "Explore Purosangue" returns to the collection pending the retailer's real

## The REDESIGN question, answered

> **Would a regular visitor recognise this as the same brand?**

Yes, and the carried elements are specific, not asserted: the prancing horse and
the `OFFICIAL` / `FERRARI DEALER` lockup taken verbatim from the sibling retailer
sites' own SVG; the retailer name in `#A6A6A6` uppercase in the position that
lockup gives it; Ferrari's near-black ground; Rosso as the only accent; tracked
uppercase grotesque as the only display voice; the American Grand Tour badge
unmodified; the brochure renders uncropped; and Ferrari's copy verbatim.
